# 🐾 Desktop Pet

> **A tiny companion that quietly lives in the corner of your desktop.**
>
> Watching. Sleeping. Eating. Exploring your daily routine.

Desktop Pet is an open-source desktop companion built with Flutter.

Unlike a traditional virtual pet, Desktop Pet becomes part of your desktop. It reacts to your activity, follows your daily routine and occasionally surprises you with small interactions that make your workspace feel alive.

---

## ✨ Features

### 💤 Daily Life

- Day and night cycle
- Sleeping behavior
- Hunger system
- Idle animations
- Emotional reactions

---

### 🎮 Activity Awareness

Your companion can react to what you're doing.

- Watches while you're gaming
- Looks at the screen while you're watching videos
- Falls asleep late at night
- Gets hungry after long sessions

---

### 🎭 Character Events

Random events make the desktop feel alive.

Examples include:

- 🍖 Friends bringing food
- 🎸 Short musical performances
- 🌊 Environmental animations
- 👋 Small surprise visits
- 💬 Funny interactions

Every event has its own appearance probability, making each day slightly different.

---

### 📦 Content Packs *(Planned)*

Desktop Pet is designed to support expandable content.

Future versions will allow adding:

- New characters
- New animations
- New events
- New behaviors

without modifying the application code.
